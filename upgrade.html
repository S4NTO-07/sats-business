Common questions

How do I get my license key after paying? +
After payment, you'll receive an email with your license key (format: SATUNO-PRO-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). Paste it in the Sats extension → Settings → License key field.
Can I pay with Bitcoin? +
Yes. Click "Pay with Lightning" to pay with sats via Lightning Network — instant, no fees. You'll need a Lightning wallet like Wallet of Satoshi, Strike, or Muun.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes, for monthly plans. Annual plans are billed once and are non-refundable after 7 days. Your key remains active until the end of the billing period.
Does Pro work on multiple devices? +
Yes. Your license key works on up to 3 devices simultaneously. Just paste the same key in Settings on each device.
