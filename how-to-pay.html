Common questions
Is Bitcoin safe to use? +
Yes. Bitcoin has been running without interruption since 2009. Lightning payments are secured cryptographically — no one can fake or reverse a payment without your approval.
What if Bitcoin's price goes down after I pay? +
The price you see at checkout is locked in at that moment. You pay the equivalent of the product's fiat price in sats — if BTC moves later, your purchase is already complete.
Are there fees? +
Lightning fees are typically less than 1 sat — fractions of a cent. For most purchases they're effectively zero. This is one of the main advantages over credit cards (2-3% fees) or bank transfers.
What's the difference between Bitcoin and Lightning? +
Bitcoin is the base layer — think of it like gold. Lightning is a payment network built on top — think of it like a debit card connected to your gold reserve. Lightning makes small, instant payments practical.
Can I get a refund? +
Lightning payments are irreversible like cash. Refund policies depend on the merchant — contact them directly if you need a refund. They can send sats back to your Lightning address.
